## Provenance of `dmd.wasm`

Built from the `wasm-web-app` branch of [`dkorpel/dmd`](https://github.com/dkorpel/dmd/tree/wasm-web-app)
— upstream `dlang/dmd` plus a WebAssembly backend and `-mwasm-selflink` — as deployed to
<https://dkorpel.github.io/dmd-explorer/>. That backend is **not** in upstream `master`, so this
asset is pinned to a branch build rather than to a DMD release. It is redistributed unmodified here;
`src/asset-receipts.js` records its SHA-256.
